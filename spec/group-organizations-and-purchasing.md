# Group Provider Organizations and Collective Purchasing

## Group provider Organizations

The Lab may propose persistent community networks or task-bounded Organizations composed of willing resource owners, gateway operators, ISPs/upstream providers, installers, maintainers, and customers. For each proposal model role and authority, resource/capacity commitments, availability, service obligations, shared expense, price, revenue allocation, minimum viable membership/capacity, failure-domain risk, support responsibility, disputes, join/withdrawal/replacement, termination and exit.

A candidate is not a member agreement. Do not list a resource as committed until owner consent, eligibility, and mandate are valid. Group formation does not mean legal incorporation or unlimited authority to bind members.

## Collective purchasing

Pool compatible demand for real offers for eligible mobile/data plans, wholesale bandwidth/backhaul, routers/access points/modems/SIM bundles, satellite equipment, towers/antennas/installation, maintenance and support. Any operator sharing/resale restrictions remain binding.

Do not assume a bulk discount exists. A real attributable offer must state supplier, product/capability, eligibility/region, quantity/minimum threshold, total and per-member cost, recurring/one-time charges and known fees/taxes, term, installation/delivery, expiry, usage/technical limitations, cancellation/refund/shortfall terms, and provenance. Estimates must be labeled.

## State machine and commitments

Use explicit states where applicable: DISCOVERED -> CANDIDATE -> INVITED -> OFFER_REQUESTED -> OFFER_RECEIVED -> VALIDATED -> COMMITMENT_OPEN -> THRESHOLD_MET -> AUTHORIZED -> ORDERED/CONTRACTED -> FULFILLED -> RECONCILED.

Failure states include EXPIRED, THRESHOLD_NOT_MET, WITHDRAWN, REJECTED, CANCELLED, FULFILLMENT_FAILED, REFUND_PENDING and DISPUTED. State transitions require authoritative event/evidence, not just UI or model claims.

Every member sees its exact allocation, fee, responsibility, term, limits, threshold, cancellation and failure outcome before committing. No order occurs before threshold and required approvals. Mandates include allowed service/amount/beneficiary scope, expiry and revocation. A member's refusal/withdrawal is handled according to stated terms.

Money movement is delegated to the authorized financial/payment authority. This product stores references and reconciled projections, not a parallel ledger/finality authority. UNKNOWN order/payment outcomes require reconciliation before retry. Discounts, costs, service credits and refunds are allocated exactly, deterministically and audibly.

## Matching and acceptance cases

Match participants by location/footprint, required performance, time window, eligible customer type, cost cap, contract term, privacy/security and risk tolerance. Do not pool incompatible intents or expose one member's private demand to other participants unnecessarily.

Acceptance: the Lab explains why a group may outperform individual purchase; a verified offer has provenance/expiry; hypothetical discount is never represented as a live quote; threshold, cancellation, refund and member approvals are enforced; duplicate order is prevented; shortfall and partial fulfillment reconcile to participant-level allocations; no resource is committed without owner authorization.
